# SignalWire calling and texting research and release gates

Research date: November 7, 2026. This is an engineering research register, not a
legal opinion or certification of compliance. **No SignalWire SDK, API client,
credentials, webhook endpoint, or live calling/texting workflow is enabled in
Railet.** Current repository support is limited to the offline, fixed-label
`customers/message_simulator.py` rehearsal (`MockMessageSubmission` /
`MockMessageEvent`).

**Effective-date and amendment caveat:** Statutory compilations and the eCFR
include provisions with differing effective dates and pending amendments (for
example, the eCFR display of `47 C.F.R. § 64.1200` as of November 5, 2026 notes
a cross-referenced amendment published at `91 FR 67219, Oct. 26, 2026`, and
Florida 2026 session bills such as SB 134 propose amendments to `§ 501.059`).
Exact effective dates, session laws, and applicability to a specific dealership
workflow remain unverified until reviewed by qualified counsel.

## 2. Key architectural conclusions from primary sources

1. **No universal consent, DNC, or hours rule:**
   - Whether prior express written consent (`47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(a)(2), (a)(3), (f)(9)`; `Fla. Stat. § 501.059(1)(g), (8)(a)`), prior express consent (`47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(a)(1)`), an established business relationship or existing-debt exemption (`47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(f)(5), (f)(15)`; `Fla. Stat. § 501.059(1)(k)`), or `§ 559.72` debt-collection rules apply depends on:
     1. The **purpose** of the communication (sales/telemarketing solicitation vs. non-marketing informational/servicing vs. consumer debt collection),
     2. The **technology** used (manual dialing/texting vs. ATDS / Florida's "automated system for the selection and dialing of telephone numbers" / artificial or prerecorded voice), and
     3. The **recipient line type and status** (wireless/cellular vs. residential landline, DNC registration, prior opt-out, or attorney representation).
2. **Unknown recipient time zone must fail closed (block automated sending / require manual review):**
   - Florida solicitation rules (`§ 501.616(6)(a)`: 8 a.m.–8 p.m. in the called person's time zone), federal solicitation rules (`47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(c)(1)`: 8 a.m.–9 p.m. local time at the called party's location), and Florida debt-collection rules (`§ 559.72(17)`: 8 a.m.–9 p.m. in the debtor's time zone, with area-code and residence presumptions in `(17)(a)–(b)` unless the caller reasonably believes the debtor's phone is in a different time zone) all depend on the **recipient's local time zone**.
   - **Control:** If a recipient's local time zone cannot be determined under the applicable statutory rule (for example, conflicting area code and residence address, or missing location data on a non-geographic number), Railet must **not** assume a single "safe" time window; automated sending must fail closed and require manual review or no send.
3. **Provider callbacks and retries cannot be trusted as sole truth or assumed unique by `(Sid, Status)`:**
   - SignalWire explicitly states that status callbacks are advisory and can be delayed or lost. Furthermore, HTTP retries or carrier updates may deliver multiple callbacks for the same `MessageSid`/`CallSid`.
   - **Control:** Log each raw webhook delivery attempt with its own ingestion ID, signature-verification result, and payload hash; derive current message/call status via an order-independent state machine; and reconcile via SignalWire REST API queries (`GET` message/call logs) before treating any delivery or opt-out state as authoritative.

## 3. Open legal, compliance, and provider questions (release gates)

- **Effective dates and 2026 legislative/regulatory changes:** Verify with counsel the effective date and text of `91 FR 67219` (amending `47 C.F.R. § 64.1200`) and the status/effective date of 2026 Florida bills affecting `§ 501.059` (such as SB 134).
- **Florida counsel classification of dealership workflows:** Obtain written legal sign-off on how Florida `§§ 501.059`, `501.604`, `501.616(6)`, and `559.72` apply to (a) sales lead follow-ups, (b) service/title/tag notifications, and (c) BHPH payment/collection contacts, including the exact applicability of `§ 559.72(18)` attorney-representation exceptions.
- **Provider sandbox verification and cost disclosure:** Verify actual SignalWire API/webhook payloads, retry behavior, 10DLC campaign registration requirements, and per-message/per-minute pass-through pricing in an isolated test environment before writing any live adapter.
- **Call recording (`Fla. Stat. § 934.03`):** Keep call recording disabled unless counsel approves two-party/all-party consent disclosures and retention controls.
